Chapter 4

Frames

 

This chapter describes the concept of frame which can be found in the frame_model schema. A frame is defined within an internal_connectivity_view or an internal_schematic_view. It may contain instances, frame_signals, frame_signal_groups and nested frames. The contents of a frame may be repeated or conditionally included in the enclosing view depending on the type of the frame. The different frame types are explained in the following sections.

4.1 For_frames

A for_frame is a frame, the contents of which are to be repeated a specified number of times during design expansion. It contains a repetition count which, when evaluated, determines the number of repetitions to be made of the frame contents. It is also associated with a for_frame_index which may be referenced in expressions within the frame.

4.2 If_frames

An if_frame is a frame, the contents of which are conditionally included during design expansion. If the specified condition evaluates to false when an if_frame is evaluated, its contents will not be included during the design expansion process.

4.3 Otherwise_frames

An otherwise_frame is a frame, the contents of which are conditionally included during design expansion if the required conditions of all related if_frames evaluate to false.

Note that a combination of if_frames and an otherwise_frame may be used to produce the equivalent of a case frame.
