21 Embedded dies

In chapter 20, a bare_board_definition is described as a re-usable bare board which supports the mounting and connection of components. In addition, it may contain a collection of embedded_dies, each of which describes a die_electrical_part embedded inside a bare board. As illustrated in figure 18.4, bare dies are placed in cavities on the substrate in the embedded die technology. It is also possible to place several dies of the same thickness on the substrate layer directly. The placement information of an embedded_die is described by the embedded_die_placement which specifies a layer or a cavity on a layer in which the die is placed. A bare board may contain a number of cavities in which dies are placed. A bare_board_cavity describes a cavity on a layer in a bare board. It specifies a layer in which a cavity is formed and also determines whether the opening of a cavity is on the top side or on the bottom side of the layer. Note that a cavity may or may not have a uniform depth (this is due to the fact that dies of different heights may be placed in the same cavity). Hence, a cavity is defined as a collection of wells (i.e. bare_board_cavity_well), each of which may have different depths. Figure 21.1 shows the model of embedded_die.
