22 Figure collectors

This chapter describes the concept of figure collectors. The models for fabricate_figure_collector and drawing_figure_collector can be found in the edif_information_model schema.

A fabricate_figure_collector describes a collector for combined geometrical shapes. The geometrical shapes may be those contributed by a set of fabricate_elements, or the result of a complex geometrical operation on a set of other fabricate_figure_collectors. The contents of a fabricate_figure_collector can only be determined in the context of a PCB; the association of fabricate_elements or other fabricate_figure_collectors with a fabricate_figure_collector only indicates which fabricate_figure_collector they will affect if they form part of a PCB. Figure 22.1 shows the model of fabricate_figure_collector.

Figure 22.1: The model of fabricate_figure_collector

A unioned_fabricate_figure_collector represents a collector which, in the context of a PCB, contains the results of a geometrical union operation performed on the geometrical contents of two or more fabricate_figure_collectors. A differenced_fabricate_figure_collector represents a collector which, in the context of a PCB, contains the results of a geometrical difference operation performed between the geometrical contents of one fabricate_figure_collector and another. A translated_fabricate_figure_collector represents a collector which, in the context of a PCB, contains the results of a geometrical translation operation performed on the geometrical contents of a fabricate_figure_collector. A rotated_fabricate_figure_collector represents a collector which, in the context of a PCB, contains the results of a geometrical rotation operation performed on the geometrical contents of a fabricate_figure_collector. A mirrored_fabricate_figure_collector represents a collector which, in the context of a PCB, contains the results of a geometrical mirroring operation on the geometrical contents of a fabricate_figure_collector. A resized_fabricate_figure_collector represents a collector which, in the context of a PCB, contains the results of a geometrical oversize or undersize of the geometrical contents of a fabricate_figure_collector.

22.1 Example of a simple artwork generation

Consider the following very simple example. A conducting layer is to consist of two separate and disjoint signal planes, each one carrying a different signal. A route is to pass through a part of both of these planes, but is required to be isolated from each plane (with different isolation spacings). Figure 22.2 shows the desired result.

Figure 22.2: Example split plane

Generating the geometry to represent this result can be achieved by the following method. Firstly, a plane describing the maximum extent of the first signal plane contributes to a simple_fabricate_figure_collector FC1. By a similar process, a plane describing the maximum extent of the second signal plane contributes to a simple_fabricate_figure_collector FC2. See figure 22.3.

Figure 22.3: Signal plane outlines

The route is described by a trace. The actual geometry of the trace contributes to three simple_fabricate_figure_collectors FC3, FC4 and FC5. The trace contains two sets of clearance geometries which form the clearance regions for the trace. One corresponds to the isolation spacing required for the first signal plane. It contributes to the simple_fabricate_figure_collector FC3. Similarly, another one which corresponds to the isolation spacing required for the second signal plane contributes to the simple_fabricate_figure_collector FC4. See figure 22.4

Figure 22.4: Trace - normal (FC5) and with clearance geometries (FC3, FC4).

Then, three fabricate_figure_collectors (FC6, FC7 and FC8) are defined. FC6 is a differenced_fabricate_figure_collector which represents the results of a geometrical difference between FC1 and FC3. FC7 is a differenced_fabricate_figure_collector which represents the results of a geometrical difference between FC2 and FC4. This gives the result as shown in figure 22.5.

Figure 22.5: Modified split plane

Finally, FC8 is a unioned_fabricate_figure_collector which represents the results of a geometrical union between FC5, FC6 and FC7. It is then contributed to the layer which represents the required split plane layer (as in figure 22.2). Note that this method of describing artwork for planes is considered to be the definitive method, and it is recommended that it be adopted as a standard way of representing such items.
