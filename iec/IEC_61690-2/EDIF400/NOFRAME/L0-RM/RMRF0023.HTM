	pcbComponentImplementationNameDef

	<pcbDieTemplatePropertyFabrication> | <pcbDrawingInformation> | 
	<pcbMountableDiePhysicalTemplatePropertyFabrication> |

	')'

Used by:

*pcbAssembledBoard

Similar Keywords:

pcbComponentImplementationRef pcbDieElectricalComponent pcbEmbeddedComponentImplementation pcbJumperComponentImplementation pcbMechanicalComponentImplementation

Constraints:

1. The following constraint applies to this context: All contexts
The template referenced by pcbMountableDiePhysicalTemplateRef must mount the same die physical template as is referenced by the part of the component referenced by pcbDieElectricalComponentRef.

Description:

Defines how the die component referenced by pcbDieElectricalComponentRef is mounted onto the bare board.

UnscaledTransform specifies the position of the component on the bare board.

PcbMountableDiePhysicalTemplateRef specifies the mountable die physical template that is used when mounting the die component on the bare board.

PcbFootprintInstanceRef specifies the footprint on which the component is placed.

PcbDieComponentTerminalImplementation describes how a mountable bond pad of the mountable die physical template is placed on the bare board.

Example:

(pcbDieComponentImplementation IMPL1_I1_1553B_TRANSVR
   (pcbDieElectricalComponentRef I1_1553B_TRANSVR)
   (pcbMountableDiePhysicalTemplateRef TOP_MNTDIE_001
      (libraryRef mountable_die_templates))
   (unscaledTransform
      (origin (pt -8636000 5588000)))
   (pcbFootprintInstanceRef FPI1)
   (pcbDieComponentTerminalImplementation TERM_001_IMP
      (pcbElectricalPartTerminalRef TERM_001)
      (pcbToeprintPadstackInstanceRef PADSTACK1
         (pcbFootprintToeprintInstanceRef FOOTPI1))
      (pcbMountableBondPadRef MBONDPAD_001) ...)
   
   (pcbDieComponentTerminalImplementation TERM_002_IMP
      (pcbElectricalPartTerminalRef TERM_002)
      (pcbToeprintPadstackInstanceRef PADSTACK2
         (pcbFootprintToeprintInstanceRef TPI1))
      (pcbMountableBondPadRef MBONDPAD_002) ...)
    ...)

pcbDieElectricalPart

pcbDieElectricalPart ::= '(' 'pcbDieElectricalPart'
	libraryObjectNameDef
	pcbPartHeader
	( pcbDiePhysicalTemplateRef | externalNoData )
	pcbDieElectricalPartType
	')'

Used by:

*external *library

Similar Keywords:

pcbDieElectricalComponent pcbDieElectricalPartFullyDefined pcbDieElectricalPartMinimallyDefined pcbDieElectricalPartRef pcbDieElectricalPartTerminal pcbDieElectricalPartTerminalConnectedBondPad pcbElectricalPartFunction pcbPackagedElectricalPart pcbPrintedElectricalPart

Constraints:

1. The following constraint applies to this context: All contexts
An electrical, connector or jumper part must have at least one terminal.
(See also similar constraint in: pcbJumperPart, pcbPackagedConnectorPart, pcbPackagedElectricalPart, pcbPrintedConnectorPart, pcbPrintedElectricalPart)

Description:

A die electrical part corresponds to a data-book description of a die, such as a 1553 transceiver.

The count of pcbDieElectricalPartTerminals defined within a pcbDieElectricalPart may be less than the count of electrical bond pads (pcbElectricalBondPad) defined within the pcbDiePhysicalTemplate that is referenced by the pcbDieElectricalPart. This means that it is possible for some of the bond pads of the die template to be referenced by none of the pcbDieElectricalPartTerminals. This does not mean that those bond pads are unconnected in the pcbDieElectricalPart; it means only that the pcbDieElectricalPart is not completely defined.

An unconnected bond pad is indicated in a pcbDieElectricalPart by defining the corresponding fully defined die electrical part terminal as having a reference to a bond pad, but no references to any part function terminals (pcbElectricalPartTerminalConnectedPortSet).

Example:

(pcbDieElectricalPart DUAL_INV
   (pcbPartHeader ...)
   (pcbDiePhysicalTemplateRef STANDARD_INTERFACE)
   (pcbDieElectricalPartFullyDefined
      (pcbElectricalPartFunction A
         (clusterRef BASIC (cellRef STINV)))
      (pcbElectricalPartFunction B
         (clusterRef BASIC (cellRef STINV)))
      (pcbElectricalPartFunctionPermutable
         (pcbElectricalPartFunctionRef A)
         (pcbElectricalPartFunctionRef B))
      (pcbDieElectricalPartTerminal T1
         (pcbElectricalPartTerminalHeader)
         (pcbDieElectricalPartTerminalConnectedBondPad
            (pcbElectricalBondPadRef BP1))
         (pcbElectricalPartTerminalConnectedPortSet
            (pcbElectricalPartFunctionPortRef a
               (pcbElectricalPartFunctionRef A))))
      (pcbDieElectricalPartTerminal T2
         (pcbElectricalPartTerminalHeader)
         (pcbDieElectricalPartTerminalConnectedBondPad
            (pcbElectricalBondPadRef BP2))
         (pcbElectricalPartTerminalConnectedPortSet
            (pcbElectricalPartFunctionPortRef y
               (pcbElectricalPartFunctionRef A))))
      (pcbDieElectricalPartTerminal T3
         (pcbElectricalPartTerminalHeader)
         (pcbDieElectricalPartTerminalConnectedBondPad
            (pcbElectricalBondPadRef BP3))
         (pcbElectricalPartTerminalConnectedPortSet
            (pcbElectricalPartFunctionPortRef GND
               (pcbElectricalPartFunctionRef A))
            (pcbElectricalPartFunctionPortRef GND
               (pcbElectricalPartFunctionRef B))))
      (pcbDieElectricalPartTerminal T4
         (pcbElectricalPartTerminalHeader)
         (pcbDieElectricalPartTerminalConnectedBondPad
            (pcbElectricalBondPadRef BP4))
         (pcbElectricalPartTerminalConnectedPortSet
            (pcbElectricalPartFunctionPortRef y
               (pcbElectricalPartFunctionRef B))))
      (pcbDieElectricalPartTerminal T5
         (pcbElectricalPartTerminalHeader)
         (pcbDieElectricalPartTerminalConnectedBondPad
            (pcbElectricalBondPadRef BP5))
         (pcbElectricalPartTerminalConnectedPortSet
            (pcbElectricalPartFunctionPortRef a
               (pcbElectricalPartFunctionRef B))))
      (pcbDieElectricalPartTerminal T6
         (pcbElectricalPartTerminalHeader)
         (pcbDieElectricalPartTerminalConnectedBondPad
            (pcbElectricalBondPadRef BP6))
         (pcbElectricalPartTerminalConnectedPortSet
            (pcbElectricalPartFunctionPortRef VCC
               (pcbElectricalPartFunctionRef A))
            (pcbElectricalPartFunctionPortRef VCC
               (pcbElectricalPartFunctionRef B))))))

This example describes the following:

         (pcbDieElectricalPartTerminalConnectedBondPad
            (pcbElectricalBondPadRef BP1)))
      (pcbDieElectricalPartTerminal T2
         (pcbElectricalPartTerminalHeader)
         (pcbDieElectricalPartTerminalConnectedBondPad
            (pcbElectricalBondPadRef BP2)))
      (pcbDieElectricalPartTerminal T3
         (pcbElectricalPartTerminalHeader)
         (pcbDieElectricalPartTerminalConnectedBondPad
            (pcbElectricalBondPadRef BP3)))
      (pcbDieElectricalPartTerminal T4
         (pcbElectricalPartTerminalHeader)
         (pcbDieElectricalPartTerminalConnectedBondPad
            (pcbElectricalBondPadRef BP4)))
      (pcbDieElectricalPartTerminal T5
         (pcbElectricalPartTerminalHeader)
         (pcbDieElectricalPartTerminalConnectedBondPad
            (pcbElectricalBondPadRef BP5)))
      (pcbDieElectricalPartTerminal T6
         (pcbElectricalPartTerminalHeader)
         (pcbDieElectricalPartTerminalConnectedBondPad
            (pcbElectricalBondPadRef BP6)))
      (pcbElectricalTerminalPermutable
         (pcbElectricalTerminalNonPermutable
            (pcbElectricalPartTerminalRef T1)
            (pcbElectricalPartTerminalRef T2))
         (pcbElectricalTerminalNonPermutable
            (pcbElectricalPartTerminalRef T3)
            (pcbElectricalPartTerminalRef T4)))))

pcbDieElectricalPartTerminal

pcbDieElectricalPartTerminal ::= '(' 'pcbDieElectricalPartTerminal'
	pcbElectricalPartTerminalNameDef
	pcbElectricalPartTerminalHeader

	')'

Similar Keywords:

pcbDieElectricalPart pcbDieElectricalPartRef pcbDieElectricalPartType pcbElectricalPartTerminalRef pcbPackagedElectricalPartTerminal pcbPrintedElectricalPartTerminal

Description:

Provides the electrical information about one of the terminals on a die electrical part and also references the corresponding bond pad in its physical template. The terminal is a logical connector out of the die electrical part.

The optional pcbElectricalPartTerminalConnectedPortSet must be used if the part is fully defined.

Example:

(pcbDieElectricalPartTerminal T1
   (pcbElectricalPartTerminalHeader)
   (pcbDieElectricalPartTerminalConnectedBondPad
      (pcbElectricalBondPadRef BP1))
   (pcbElectricalPartTerminalConnectedPortSet
      (pcbElectricalPartFunctionPortRef a
         (pcbElectricalPartFunctionRef A))))

Here, the terminal T1 is defined. This corresponds to bond pad BP1 in the die's physical template. The terminal is connected to the a connection of function A defined in the fully-defined part.

pcbDieEnvironmentalConditions

pcbDieEnvironmentalConditions ::= '(' 'pcbDieEnvironmentalConditions'
	temperatureRange
	pressureRange
	voltageDifferential
	relativeHumidity
	maximumDuration
	')'

Used by:

!pcbDieAttachmentConditions !pcbDieBondingConditions !pcbDieSealingConditions

Similar Keywords:

pcbDieStorageConditions

Description:

Specifies the environmental conditions that should be adhered to when a die is sealed, bonded or attached, and the maximum duration of these conditions.

PressureRange is the pressure that is acting upon the die during the sealing, bonding or attachment process.

VoltageDifferential is the maximum voltage differential between the die's power pads when it is normally powered-up and when it is undergoing the sealing, bonding or attachment process.

Example:

(pcbDieAttachmentConditions
   (pcbDieEnvironmentalConditions
      (temperatureRange (mnm -20 20 40))
      (pressureRange (mnm (e 5 -1) 1 (e 15 -1)))
      (voltageDifferential 20)
      (relativeHumidity 40)
      (maximumDuration 5)))

Description:

Defines a die fiducial, which is used to identify the position of a die, e.g. for autobonding machines. The die fiducial is formed by referencing a die fiducial template. (pcbDiePhysicalTemplate STANDARD_INTERFACE

      (pcbLayoutUnits)
      (nameInformation (primaryName "DIE_FCH_7486")))
   (pcbDieFiducial FIDUCIAL1
      (pcbDieFiducialTemplateRef DFT1)
      (fixedScaleTransform (origin (pt 100 200)))
      (pcbDieSide (pcbDieSideTop))))
