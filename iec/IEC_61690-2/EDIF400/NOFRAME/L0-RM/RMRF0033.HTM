pcbFigureCollectorDefinitions

pcbFigureCollectorDefinitions ::= '(' 'pcbFigureCollectorDefinitions'
	{ pcbDifferencedFabricateFigureCollector | pcbDrawingFigureCollector | 
	pcbMirroredFabricateFigureCollector | pcbResizedFabricateFigureCollector 
	| pcbRotatedFabricateFigureCollector | pcbSimpleFabricateFigureCollector | 
	pcbTranslatedFabricateFigureCollector | 
	pcbUnionedFabricateFigureCollector } 
	')'

Used by:

<edifHeader

Similar Keywords:

figure pcbReferencedFigureCollector

Description:

Contains the definitions of figure collectors for a set of EDIF data. PcbDrawingFigureCollector is used to collect drawing data. The other figure collectors can form part of the fabrication of an mcm or pcb.

Each pcbSimpleFabricateFigureCollector collects geometric primitives defined in a design. The other fabricate figure collectors allow for combinations of figure collectors to achieve a desired piece of artwork, i.e they represent the definition of how a two-dimensional geometric shape is constructed from other two-dimensional geometric shapes.

For example, a figure collector named A could be defined as the union of figure collectors B and C. A, B and C could correspond to layers L1, L2 and L3 on the board, which means that the geometric shapes on L1 are derived automatically from the union of L2 and L3.

Example:

(pcbFigureCollectorDefinitions
   (pcbSimpleFabricateFigureCollector GG1)
   (pcbSimpleFabricateFigureCollector GG2)
   (pcbSimpleFabricateFigureCollector GG5)
   (pcbResizedFabricateFigureCollector GG3
      (pcbFabricateFigureCollectorRef GG5)
      (resizeDistance 100)
      (cornerType (round)))
   (pcbResizedFabricateFigureCollector GG4
      (pcbFabricateFigureCollectorRef GG3)
      (resizeDistance 100)
      (cornerType (round)))
   (pcbDifferencedFabricateFigureCollector GG6
      (pcbFabricateFigureCollectorRef GG1)
      (pcbFabricateFigureCollectorRef GG3))
   (pcbDifferencedFabricateFigureCollector GG7
      (pcbFabricateFigureCollectorRef GG2)
      (pcbFabricateFigureCollectorRef GG4))
   (pcbUnionedfabricateFigureCollector GG8
      (pcbFabricateFigureCollectorRef GG5)
      (pcbFabricateFigureCollectorRef GG6)
      (pcbFabricateFigureCollectorRef GG7)))

A layer is to consist of two separate and disjoint signal planes; each one carries a different signal. A route is to pass through a part of both of these planes, but is required to be isolated from each plane (with different isolation spacings).

Split plane; can be defined by using figure collector GG8

To create the above artwork, the following needs to be done:

1. Associate each positive-image signal plane and route layer with a figure collector, as shown below.

2. Oversize GG5 to produce the following.

3. Oversize GG3 to produce the following.

Note that in more complex examples it would be easier to define simple figure collectors for GG3 and GG4. The geometry that goes into them could be a clearance around GG5 (using pcbClearanceGeometry).

4. Perform a difference operation on GG1 and GG3 to produce the following.

5. Perform a difference operation on GG2 and GG4 to produce the following.

6. Perform a union operation on GG5, GG6 and GG7 to produce the final artwork.

      (pcbNonSpecificSubLayoutDefinitionRef SMD_PAD)
      (fixedScaleTransform ...)))

This example describes a simple footprint for a surface-mount resistor.

pcbHoleClearanceRule

pcbHoleClearanceRule ::= '(' 'pcbHoleClearanceRule'
	pcbRuleNameDef
	pcbFirstHoleInformation
	pcbSecondHoleInformation
	pcbMinimumHoleClearance
	{ property } 
	')'

Used by:

*pcbBareBoardTechnologyRule

Description:

Specifies the minimum clearance between holes. This is to ensure that holes are not so close together that they compromise the structural integrity of the board. The distance is measured between the closest points of the holes.

Different clearance rules can be specified for the following:

* Between holes that conform to a specified drill rule and holes that conform to another specified drill rule.

* Between holes that conform to a specified drill rule and any other hole.

* Between any holes.

Example:

(pcbBareBoardTechnologyRule BBTR
   (pcbTechnologyRuleHeader ...)
   (pcbTechnologyRuleDetails ...)
   (pcbHoleClearanceRule FHCR
      (pcbAnyHole)
      (pcbDrillRuleRef DR1)
      (pcbMinimumHoleClearance 10)))

In this example, the minimum clearance between a hole that conforms to drill rule DR1 and any other hole is 10 units.
