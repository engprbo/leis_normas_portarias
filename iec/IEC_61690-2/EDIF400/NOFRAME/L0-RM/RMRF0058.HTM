signal

signal ::= '(' 'signal'
	signalNameDef
	signalJoined
	{ <nameInformation> | property } 
	')'

Used by:

*logicalConnectivity

Similar Keywords:

extendSignalDef extendSignalGroupDef joinedAsSignal pcbComponentSignal pcbComponentSignalLayerRealizationRule pcbComponentSignalNameCaseSensitive pcbComponentSignalNameDef pcbComponentSignalNameRef pcbComponentSignalRealizationRule pcbComponentSignalRef pcbComponentSignalSet pcbNoComponentSignal pcbPadIntendedUseSignalPositive signalAnnotate signalGroup signalGroupAnnotate signalGroupNameCaseSensitive signalGroupNameDef signalGroupNameRef signalGroupRef signalList signalNameCaseSensitive signalNameRef signalRef

Constraints:

1. This construct is illegal in this context: external

Description:

A signal is a named object which lists ALL the ports, master, instance and global, which are electrically common at a given level of hierarchy. A signal definition applies to an entire view.

A signal is always one bit wide in Level 0.

The signals define all the connectivity at a given level of hierarchy within a view.

Example:

(signal SIG1 
   (signalJoined 
      (portRef MP1) 
      (portInstanceRef P1 (instanceRef I1))))

In this example, the signal SIG1 joins the master port MP1 and the port P1 of instance I1.

signalGroup

signalGroup ::= '(' 'signalGroup'
	signalGroupNameDef
	signalList
	{ <nameInformation> | property } 
	')'

Used by:

*logicalConnectivity

Similar Keywords:

extendSignalGroupDef signal signalGroupAnnotate signalGroupNameCaseSensitive signalGroupNameRef signalGroupRef

Constraints:

1. The following constraint applies to this context: All contexts
No two signal groups in the same context should have the same list of members

2. This construct is illegal in this context: external

Description:

A signalGroup is a named object which provides a grouping mechanism for signals. This provides a basis for the structured connectivity established by busses by allowing a hierarchical grouping mechanism for signals.

A signalGroup does not establish connectivity; it has no signalJoined.

A signalGroup is defined in terms of an ordered list of one or more signals or signalGroups. No two signalGroups may have the same list of members, although when flattened, two signalGroups may possibly resolve to the same ordered list of signals. Thus, different signalGroups will have different structure.

A signalGroup has a width that is equal to the sum of the widths of its constituent members.

The same signal or signal group may be referenced in the signalList of more than one signalGroup.

The same signal or signal group may be referenced more than once in a single signalGroup definition.

SignalGroups define their structure in terms of the signals and signalGroups that they reference. This structure is independent of the port ordering or port structure.

Example:

(signal OP_0 ...)
(signal OP_1 ...)
(signal OP_2 ...)
(signal OP_3 ...)
(signalGroup OP_0_1 
   (signalList (signalRef OP_0) (signalRef OP_1)))
(signalGroup OP_2_3 
   (signalList (signalRef OP_2) (signalRef OP_3)))
(signalGroup OP 
   (signalList (signalRef OP_0_1) (signalRef OP_2_3)))

In this example, four signals are defined and then grouped. The first two, OP_0 and OP_1, are grouped into one signalGroup, OP_0_1. The second two, OP_2 and OP_3, are grouped into another signalGroup, OP_2_3. Finally a signalGroup OP is defined as a grouping of the previously-defined signalGroups OP_0_1 and OP_2_3.

specialCharacter

'!' | '#' | '$' | '&' | ''' | '(' | ')' | '*' | '+' | ',' | '-' | '.' | '/' | ':' | ';' | '<' | '=' | '>' | '?' | '@' | '[' | '\' | 
']' | '^' | '_' | '`' | '{' | '|' | '}' | '~'

Used by:

stringToken

Similar Keywords:

character

Description:

These are the punctuation characters that are legal within string tokens.

The quote and percent characters are explicitly excluded from this list, as they have special significance within stringTokens. If required, such characters must be expressed with an encodedCharacter escape sequence.

Example:

"!#$&'()*+,-./:;<=>?@[\]}^_`|~"
"%37%"
"%34%"

The last two strings represent strings each with a single character, % (percent) in the first and " (quote) in the second.
