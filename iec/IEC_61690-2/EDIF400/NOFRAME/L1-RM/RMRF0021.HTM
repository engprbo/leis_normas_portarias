	{ pcbPhysicalLayer } 
	')'

Constraints:

1. The following constraint applies to this context: All contexts
There must be at least one item (in addition to any required items) in the construct.
(See also similar constraint in: closedAreaCutout, closedAreaElementSet, complexName, geometryElementSet, globalPortList, pcbAllocatedPartFunctionPortSet, pcbAllowedRotations, pcbClearanceGeometry, pcbComponentSet, pcbComponentSignalSet, pcbComponentTerminalJoined, pcbComponentTerminalList, pcbComponentTerminalSet, pcbContributesToDrawing, pcbContributesToFabrication, pcbDieBondSites, pcbDimensionedObjectSet, pcbDrawingAnnotationSet, pcbDrawingBorderTemplateCenteringMark, pcbDrawingBorderTemplateOrientationMark, pcbDrillRuleFromLayer, pcbDrillRuleLayerSet, pcbDrillRuleSet, pcbDrillRuleToLayer, pcbFabricateTextAppearanceAdd, pcbFabricateTextAppearanceReplace, pcbFabricateTextImplementation, pcbFabricateTextSet, pcbIncludedDrawingFigureCollectorSet, pcbIncludedFabricateFigureCollectorSet, pcbMaterialList, pcbMountableBondPadSet, pcbMountablePackageSet, pcbNonPrintedComponentSet, pcbPackagePinGroupPins, pcbPadRoutingTarget, pcbPhysicalLayerSet, pcbPhysicalTextSet, pcbPropertyNameDrawing, pcbPropertyValueDrawing, pcbTextAppearanceSet, portList, replaceDisplay, signalList, unfilledOrOpenElementSet)

Description:

Provides details of the physical layers on a bare board.

A physical layer is either a sheet of material that is applied to a layer of dielectric or other material, or the dielectric material itself.

Example:

(pcbBareBoardLayers
   (pcbPhysicalLayer SUBSTRATE
      (pcbPhysicalLayerHeader)
      (pcbNonConductingLayer
         (pcbNonConductingLayerSubstrate))
      (pcbIsMadeOfMaterial
         (pcbMaterialRef EpoxyGlass))
      (pcbReferencedFigureCollector
         (pcbFabricateFigureCollectorRef FC1))
      (thickness (mnm 2 3 4))) ...)

pcbBareBoardLayerTechnologyRule

pcbBareBoardLayerTechnologyRule ::= '(' 'pcbBareBoardLayerTechnologyRule'
	pcbTechnologyRuleNameDef
	pcbTechnologyRuleHeader
	pcbPhysicalLayerSet
	pcbTechnologyRuleDetails
	{ <pcbTechnologyRuleRoutingStrategy> } 
	')'

Used by:

*pcbBareBoardTechnologyRule

Similar Keywords:

pcbBareBoard pcbBareBoardRegionTechnologyRule pcbBareBoardTechnology technology

Constraints:

1. The following constraint applies to this context: All contexts
A given layer may be referenced at most once by a pcbBareBoardLayerTechnologyRule.

Description:

Overrides one or more bare board technology clearance rules for specific layers of the bare board. In addition, it may also be used to specify a routing strategy for the layers.

PcbPhysicalLayerSet specifies the layers that the layer-specific rules apply to.

PcbTechnologyRuleDetails gives details of the rules that apply.

Example:

(pcbBareBoardTechnologyRule BBTR
   (pcbTechnologyRuleHeader ...)
   (pcbTechnologyRuleDetails
      (pcbMinimumTraceToTrace 20) ...)
   (pcbBareBoardLayerTechnologyRule BBRL2
      (pcbTechnologyRuleHeader ...)
      (pcbPhysicalLayerSet
         (pcbPhysicalLayerRef LAYER2))
      (pcbTechnologyRuleDetails
         (pcbMinimumTraceToTrace 30))))

In this example, the minimum trace-to-trace rule is overridden for LAYER2.

pcbBareBoardRegionTechnologyRule

pcbBareBoardRegionTechnologyRule ::= '(' 'pcbBareBoardRegionTechnologyRule'
	pcbTechnologyRuleNameDef
	pcbTechnologyRuleHeader
	pcbPhysicalLayerSet
	closedAreaElement
	pcbTechnologyRuleDetails
	{ <pcbKeepInItems> | <pcbKeepOutItems> | 
	<pcbTechnologyRuleRoutingStrategy> } 
	')'

Used by:

*pcbBareBoardConstraint

Similar Keywords:

pcbBareBoard pcbBareBoardLayerTechnologyRule pcbBareBoardTechnology pcbBareBoardTechnologyRule technology

Constraints:

1. The following constraint applies to this context: All contexts
No item may be referenced by both the pcbKeepInItems and the pcbKeepOutItems.

Description:

Overrides one or more bare board technology clearance rules for a specific region of the bare board. In addition, it may also be used to specify keep-in or keep-out areas for various automated activities (e.g. autorouting), plus a routing strategy for the region.

PcbPhysicalLayerSet specifies the layers that the region-specific rules apply to.

ClosedAreaElement specifies the region of the bare board.

PcbTechnologyRuleDetails gives details of the new rules that apply in the region.

Example:

(pcbBareBoardConstraint BBC
   (pcbTechnologyRuleRef RR1)
   (pcbBareBoardRegionTechnologyRule TTTL2TR
      (pcbTechnologyRuleHeader ...)
      (pcbPhysicalLayerSet
         (pcbPhysicalLayerRef LAYER2))
      (rectangle (pt 100 200) (pt 500 500))
      (pcbTechnologyRuleDetails
         (pcbMinimumTraceToTrace 20))) ...)

In this example, the minimum trace-to-trace rule is overridden within a rectangular closed area element on LAYER2.

   (pcbStackedLayerGroup ...)
   (pcbDrillRule ...))

pcbBareBoardTechnologyRule ::= '(' 'pcbBareBoardTechnologyRule'
	pcbTechnologyRuleNameDef
	pcbTechnologyRuleHeader
	pcbTechnologyRuleDetails
	{ pcbBareBoardLayerTechnologyRule | <pcbCavitySize> | 
	<pcbCavityToBoardEdgeSpacing> | <pcbCavityToCavitySpacing> |

	pcbHoleClearanceRule | <pcbHoleToCavitySpacing> | 
	<pcbMaximumDrillingTolerance> | <pcbMaximumPinPlaceHoleSize> |

	<pcbMaximumViaHoleSize> | <pcbMetalArtifactToCavitySpacing> | 
	<pcbMinimumPinPlaceHoleSize> | <pcbMinimumPlatedHoleSize> | 
	<pcbMinimumUnplatedHoleSize> | <pcbMinimumViaHoleSize> | 
	<pcbTechnologyRuleViasAllowed> | <pcbTraceWidthIntoCavity> | 
	pcbViaGrid } 
	')'

Used by:

*pcbBareBoardTechnology

Similar Keywords:

pcbBareBoard pcbBareBoardRegionTechnologyRule pcbBareBoardTechnologyHeader pcbBareBoardTechnologyRef technology

Description:

Specifies the bare board technology rules (clearance and manufacturing) for the bare board as a whole. The clearance rules are specified in pcbTechnologyRuleDetails and may be overridden for specific regions of the bare board by using pcbBareBoardRegionTechnologyRule.

PcbBareBoardLayerTechnologyRule enables some of the rules to be overridden for specified layers of the bare board.

1 = pcbCavityToBoardEdgeSpacing

2 = pcbCavityToCavitySpacing

3 = pcbHoleToCavitySpacing

4 = pcbMetalArtifactToCavitySpacing

5 = pcbCavitySize

6 = pcbCavityWellWallAngle

7 =pcbTraceWidthIntoCavity

8 = pcbDieToCavityWellWallSpacing

9 = pcbDieToCavityWellEdgeSpacing

10 = pcbDieToLayerSurfaceTolerance

11 = pcbDieToDieHeightTolerance

13 = pcbDieToDieSpacing

14 = pcbDiePositionToleranceWidth

15 = pcbDieBondPadToDieEdgeSpacing

Example:

(pcbBareBoardTechnology TT4
   (pcbBareBoardTechnologyHeader ...)
   (pcbBareBoardLayers ...)
   (pcbStackedLayerGroup ...)
   (pcbBareBoardTechnologyRule BBTR
      (pcbTechnologyRuleHeader)
      (pcbTechnologyRuleDetails
         (pcbMinimumTraceToTrace 20) ...)
      (pcbMaximumPinPlaceHoleSize 10) ...) ...)

pcbBoardSubsetDefinition

pcbBoardSubsetDefinition ::= '(' 'pcbBoardSubsetDefinition'
	pcbBoardSubsetNameDef
	pcbBoardSubsetDefinitionHeader
	{ pcbBoardSubsetConstructionFigure | pcbComponentSymbolSelection | 
	pcbDimensionedArc | pcbDimensionedCircle | pcbDimensionedLine | 
	pcbDimensionedPoint | pcbDrillSymbolSelection | 
	<pcbIncludedDrawingFigureCollectorSet> | 
	<pcbIncludedFabricateFigureCollectorSet> } 
	')'

Used by:

*pcbAssembledBoard *pcbBareBoardDefinition

Similar Keywords:

pcbBoardSubsetPlacement pcbBoardSubsetRef

Constraints:

1. This construct is illegal in this context: pcbBareBoardDefinition <external

Description:

Identifies a subset of the items that describe some aspect of a board, e.g. traces and component outlines, that are to be visible on a drawing and specifies the items (i.e. arcs, circles, lines and points) that are to be dimensioned. Only the construction figures, selected symbols and all layout features on the included figure collectors will be visible on the drawing.

A board subset may be placed in one or more drawings. All the distance values in the board subset definition are in the distance units of the pcbMcmView, as defined by setDistance.

Example:

(pcbBoardSubsetDefinition BS1
   (pcbBoardSubsetDefinitionHeader 
      (pcbClippingOutline
         (unfilledRectangle (pt 0 0) (pt 200 100))))
   (pcbIncludedDrawingFigureCollectorSet ...)
   (pcbBoardSubsetConstructionFigure CF1
      (geometryElementSet
         (path (pointList (pt 1000 1000) (pt 2000 1000)))
         (path (pointList (pt 1000 2000) (pt 2000 2000)))))
   (pcbDimensionedLine DimLine1
      (lineSegment (pt 1000 1000) (pt 2000 1000))
      (pcbDimensionedObjectSet
         (pcbBoardSubsetConstructionFigureRef CF1)))
   (pcbDimensionedLine DimLine2
      (lineSegment (pt 1000 2000) (pt 2000 2000))
      (pcbDimensionedObjectSet
         (pcbBoardSubsetConstructionFigureRef CF1)))
   (pcbDimensionedPoint DimPnt1
      (pt 10 100)
      (pcbDimensionedObjectSet
         (pcbBareBoardProbePointRef ProbeP23))) ...)

This simple example defines the construction figure CF1. The paths within CF1 are identified as being dimensioned lines by DimLine1 and DimLine2. Also included in the pcbBoardSubsetDefinition is a dimensioned point, DimPnt1. Of course, the pcbBoardSubsetDefinition is likely to contain many more dimensioned items on the board.

Example:

(pcbBoardSubsetExtraDetail BS1

      (geometryElementSet 
         (path (pointList (pt 1000 1000) (pt 2000 1000)))
         (path (pointList (pt 1000 2000) (pt 2000 2000)))))
   (pcbBoardSubsetExtraDetailFigure FIG1
      (pcbDrawingFigure DEFAULT_ATTRS
         (geometryElementSet 
            (polygon 
               (pointList (pt 0 100) (pt 100 400) (pt 0 300)))))) ...)

pcbBoardSubsetPlacement

pcbBoardSubsetPlacement ::= '(' 'pcbBoardSubsetPlacement'
	pcbBoardSubsetPlacementNameDef
	pcbBoardSubsetRef
	uniformScaleTransform
	( pcbBoardSubsetPlacementScaled | pcbBoardSubsetPlacementUnscaled )
	{ <pcbClippingOutlineDrawn> | pcbDrawingFigure } 
	')'

Used by:

*pcbDrawing

Similar Keywords:

pcbBoardSubsetDefinition pcbBoardSubsetPlacementNameRef pcbBoardSubsetPlacementRef

Constraints:

1. The following constraint applies to this context: All contexts
The absolute values of scaleX and scaleY within transform must be equal.

Description:

Defines how a board subset is placed on a drawing.

Transform specifies the position of the board subset on the drawing. Units are as specified in pcbDrawingUnits in pcbDrawingHeader.

The board subset can be scaled by using pcbBoardSubsetPlacementScaled.

PcbClippingOutlineDrawn indicates whether the clipping outline should be displayed on the drawing.

PcbDrawingFigure can describe a collection of geometrical shapes that are associated with the board subset placement.

Example:

(pcbBoardSubsetPlacement BSP1
   (pcbBoardSubsetRef BS1)
   (uniformScaleTransform ...)
   (pcbBoardSubsetPlacementUnscaled) ...)
