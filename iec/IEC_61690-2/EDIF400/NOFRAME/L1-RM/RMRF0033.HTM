2. The following constraint applies to this context: All contexts

3. The following constraint applies to this context: All contexts
Each layer referenced in pcbDrillRuleFromLayer and pcbDrillRuleLayerSet must be closer to the top of the board than any of the layers referenced in each subsequent pcbDrillRuleLayerSet or pcbDrillRuleToLayer.

4. This construct is illegal in this context: external

Description:

References the layer(s) that a drilled hole conforming to the enclosing drill rule must start from, go to and pass through.

When a pcbDrillRuleFromLayer references more than one layer, the drilled hole may start from any of the layers referenced in that construct. Similarly, if a pcbDrillRuleToLayer references more than one layer, the drilled hole may go to any of the layers referenced in that construct. Also, if a pcbDrillRuleLayerSet within the pcbDrillRuleThroughLayerList references more than one layer, the drilled hole may go through any of the layers referenced in that construct.

A layer referenced in pcbDrillRuleFromLayer must not be coplanar with any layer referenced in pcbDrillRuleToLayer nor with any other layer referenced in pcbDrillRuleThroughLayerList.

Example:

(pcbDrillRuleLayers
   (pcbDrillRuleFromLayer 
      (pcbPhysicalLayerRef LAYER1))
   (pcbDrillRuleToLayer 
      (pcbPhysicalLayerRef LAYER2)))
