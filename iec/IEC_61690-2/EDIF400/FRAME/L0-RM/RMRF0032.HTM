Description:

Specifies the fabrication of text. PcbContributesToFabrication indicates the simple figure collectors that the graphics are assigned to and each pcbPhysicalTextImplementation represents the implementation of the text.

Example:

(pcbFabricateTextImplementation
    (pcbContributesToFabrication
      (pcbSimpleFabricateFigureCollectorRef SILK_1)
      (pcbSimpleFabricateFigureCollectorRef SILK_2))
   (pcbPhysicalTextImplementation
      ...))
