(See also similar constraint in: pcbElectricalPartFunctionNonPermutable, pcbElectricalTerminalNonPermutable, pcbElectricalTerminalPermutable)

Description:

Describes a relationship in which functions are interchangeable; for example, AND gates within a packaged or printed electrical part. PcbElectricalPartFunctionPermutable may be nested with pcbElectricalPartFunctionNonPermutable to specify complex relationships between functions in the part.

In the context of a pcbElectricalPartFunctionPermutable construct, corresponding functions may be exchanged as a whole or not at all; partial exchanges are not permitted. For instance, in the second example shown below, E is permutable with F only if G and H are swapped at the same time. As you can see from this example, the order of pcbElectricalPartFunctionRefs within pcbElectricalPartFunctionNonPermutable is significant.

Example:

(pcbElectricalPartFunctionPermutable
   (pcbElectricalPartFunctionNonPermutable
      (pcbElectricalPartFunctionPermutable 
         (pcbElectricalPartFunctionRef A)
         (pcbElectricalPartFunctionRef B))
      (pcbElectricalPartFunctionPermutable
         (pcbElectricalPartFunctionRef C)
         (pcbElectricalPartFunctionRef D))))

This describes the permutability of the following part. A and B are permutable, and C and D are permutable. However, A is not permutable with C and B is not permutable with D. See nonPermutable for a further example.

Example:

(pcbElectricalPartFunctionPermutable
   (pcbElectricalPartFunctionNonPermutable 
      (pcbElectricalPartFunctionRef E)
      (pcbElectricalPartFunctionRef G))
   (pcbElectricalPartFunctionNonPermutable
      (pcbElectricalPartFunctionRef F)
      (pcbElectricalPartFunctionRef H)))

This describes the permutability of the following part. E and G taken together are permutable with F and H taken together. However, E is not permutable with G, nor is F permutable with H. See nonPermutable for a further example.

Constraints:

1. The following constraint applies to this context: All contexts
A portNameRef within pcbElectricalPartFunctionPortRef may only be used once within the set of pcbElectricalPartTerminalConnectedPortSet within an electrical part. (There is only one part_function_terminal for a particular master_port on a part_function.)

Description:

References a set of ports, each belonging to a function located within a packaged, printed or die electrical part.

Example:

(pcbElectricalPartFunction D
   (clusterRef BASIC (cellRef STINV)))
(pcbPackagedElectricalPartTerminal T8 ...
   (pcbElectricalPartTerminalConnectedPortSet
      (pcbElectricalPartFunctionPortRef y
         (pcbElectricalPartFunctionRef D))))

Here, the part contains a function D. The y port of the function is connected to T8 (terminal 8) of the part.
