	{ <pcbDimensionedObjectSet> } 
	')'

Used by:

Similar Keywords:

arc pcbDimensionedArcRef

Description:

Defines a hook for a pcbDimensionCircularRadial dimension to refer to. In this context, the pcbDimensionedArc defines the arc being measured.

ArcByCenter is not required to correspond to any real feature referenced in the pcbDimensionedObjectSet. For example, it may be an imaginary arc implied by the existence of two or more real features, or it may be that the system generating the EDIF description does not know the association of the dimensioned arc with any real features in the design.

Example:

(pcbDimensionedArc DimArc1
   (arcByCenter (pt 5 15) (pt 10 15) (pt 15 15) 
      (arcDirectionClockwise (false)))
   (pcbDimensionedObjectSet
      (pcbDimensionedObjectBareBoardOutline)))
