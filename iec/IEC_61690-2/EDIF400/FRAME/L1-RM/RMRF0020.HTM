pcbAssembledPhysicalNets

pcbAssembledPhysicalNets ::= '(' 'pcbAssembledPhysicalNets'
	( pcbAnalyzedAssembledPhysicalNets | 
	pcbNonAnalyzedAssembledPhysicalNets )
	')'

Used by:

<pcbAssembledBoard

Description:

Describes how the physical nets on the bare board are assembled. In other words, it describes the connectivity between physical nets on the bare board.

The assembled physical nets are categorised as either analyzed or not analyzed to specify whether or not the sending system has checked their connectivity against the connectivity of signals.

An assembled physical net is a collection of instance nets on the bare board, electrical component terminals and jumpers that are intended to be electrically connected and electrically disjoint from other assembled physical nets. EDIF does not guarantee that an assembled physical net fulfils this criterion.

Example:

(pcbAssembledPhysicalNets
   (pcbAnalyzedAssembledPhysicalNets
      (pcbCorrectNet NET_FOR_SIG1
         (pcbAssembledPhysicalNetHeader)
         (pcbComponentSignalRef SIG1)
         (pcbInstanceNets
            (pcbSubLayoutInstancePhysicalNetRef 
               NET1 (pcbSubLayoutInstanceRef BB))
            (pcbSubLayoutInstancePhysicalNetRef
               NET2 (pcbSubLayoutInstanceRef BB)))
         (pcbJumperConnection JUMPER1
            (pcbJumperConnectionHeader)
            (pcbJumperConnectionDiscreteWire
               (pcbJumperConnectionDiscreteWireConnectionPoints
                  (pcbPadstackInstanceRef JPAD1)
                  (pcbPadstackInstanceRef JPAD2)))))))

In this example, the connectivity defined by SIG1 is implemented by NET1, NET2 and a jumper, JUMPER1. JUMPER1 could be the physical mechanism that is used to join the two physical nets together.

Constraints:

1. The following constraint applies to this context: All contexts
A pcbBareBoardCavity must have at least one pcbBareBoardCavityWell.

2. This construct is illegal in this context: external

Description:

A bare board cavity is a cavity on a layer of a bare board in which dies are placed. A cavity can contain more than one bare board cavity well, as specified by pcbBareBoardCavityWell, which may not have uniform depths. See diagram under the keyword pcbBareBoardTechnologyRule.

PcbPhysicalLayerRef specifies the physical layer in which a cavity is formed.

PcbLayerSide specifies whether the cavity is formed on the top side or bottom side of the layer.

ClosedAreaElement specifies the surface area of the cavity.

Example:

(pcbBareBoardCavity BBCAV1
   (pcbPhysicalLayerRef LAYER2)
   (pcbLayerSide (pcbLayerSideTop))
   (rectangle (pt 0 0)(pt 8000 6000))
   (pcbBareBoardCavityWell WELL1
      (depth (mnm 500 600 700))
      (pcbBareBoardCavityWellDiePlacementArea
         (rectangle (pt 1000 1000)(pt 3000 5000))))
   (pcbBareBoardCavityWell WELL2
      (depth (mnm 500 600 700))
      (pcbBareBoardCavityWellDiePlacementArea
         (rectangle (pt 5000 1000)(pt 7000 5000)))))

In this example, the cavity has two wells: WELL1 and WELL2.
