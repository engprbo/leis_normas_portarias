pcbFigureCollectorDefinitions ::= '(' 'pcbFigureCollectorDefinitions'
	{ pcbDifferencedFabricateFigureCollector | pcbDrawingFigureCollector | 
	pcbMirroredFabricateFigureCollector | pcbResizedFabricateFigureCollector

	pcbUnionedFabricateFigureCollector }

Used by:

Similar Keywords:

figure pcbReferencedFigureCollector

Description:

Contains the definitions of figure collectors for a set of EDIF data. PcbDrawingFigureCollector is used to collect drawing data. The other figure collectors can form part of the fabrication of an mcm or pcb.

Each pcbSimpleFabricateFigureCollector collects geometric primitives defined in a design. The other fabricate figure collectors allow for combinations of figure collectors to achieve a desired piece of artwork, i.e they represent the definition of how a two-dimensional geometric shape is constructed from other two-dimensional geometric shapes.

For example, a figure collector named A could be defined as the union of figure collectors B and C. A, B and C could correspond to layers L1, L2 and L3 on the board, which means that the geometric shapes on L1 are derived automatically from the union of L2 and L3.

Example:

(pcbFigureCollectorDefinitions
   (pcbSimpleFabricateFigureCollector GG1)
   (pcbSimpleFabricateFigureCollector GG2)
   (pcbSimpleFabricateFigureCollector GG5)
   (pcbResizedFabricateFigureCollector GG3
      (pcbFabricateFigureCollectorRef GG5)
      (resizeDistance 100)
      (cornerType (round)))
   (pcbResizedFabricateFigureCollector GG4
      (pcbFabricateFigureCollectorRef GG3)
      (resizeDistance 100)
      (cornerType (round)))
   (pcbDifferencedFabricateFigureCollector GG6
      (pcbFabricateFigureCollectorRef GG1)
      (pcbFabricateFigureCollectorRef GG3))
   (pcbDifferencedFabricateFigureCollector GG7
      (pcbFabricateFigureCollectorRef GG2)
      (pcbFabricateFigureCollectorRef GG4))
   (pcbUnionedfabricateFigureCollector GG8
      (pcbFabricateFigureCollectorRef GG5)
