A pcbComponentSignal may be implemented by one and only one of the following combinations: 1. By zero, one or more pcbNonAnalyzedAssembledPhysicalNets, 2. By exactly one pcbCorrectNet, 3. By zero, one or more pcbIncorrectNet, 4. By zero, one or more pcbShortingNet.

         NET2 (pcbSubLayoutInstanceRef BB)))

      (pcbJumperConnectionDiscreteWire
         (pcbJumperConnectionDiscreteWireConnectionPoints
            (pcbPadstackInstanceRef JPAD1)
            (pcbPadstackInstanceRef JPAD2)))))

In this example, the connectivity defined by SIG1 is implemented by NET1, NET2 and a jumper, JUMPER1. JUMPER1 could be the physical mechanism that is used to join the two physical nets together.

pcbDieComponentTerminalImplementation

	pcbComponentTerminalImplementationNameDef

Used by:

*pcbDieComponentImplementation

Similar Keywords:

pcbComponentTerminalImplementationRef pcbJumperComponentTerminalImplementation

Description:

Describes how a mountable bond pad is placed on the bare board.

PcbElectricalPartTerminalRef is a reference to the corresponding terminal in the die's part. This could be derived, but is also stated here for convenience.

Example:

(pcbDieComponentImplementation IMPL1_I1_1553B_TRANSVR
   (pcbDieElectricalComponentRef I1_1553B_TRANSVR)
   (pcbMountableDiePhysicalTemplateRef TOP_MNTDIE_001
      (libraryRef mountable_die_templates))
   (unscaledTransform
      (origin (pt -8636000 5588000)))
   (pcbFootprintInstanceRef FPI1)
   (pcbDieComponentTerminalImplementation TERM_001_IMP
      (pcbElectricalPartTerminalRef TERM_001)
      (pcbToeprintPadstackInstanceRef PADSTACK1
         (pcbFootprintToeprintInstanceRef FOOTPI1))
      (pcbMountableBondPadRef MBONDPAD_001) ...)
   
   (pcbDieComponentTerminalImplementation TERM_002_IMP
      (pcbElectricalPartTerminalRef TERM_002)
      (pcbToeprintPadstackInstanceRef PADSTACK2
         (pcbFootprintToeprintInstanceRef TPI1))
      (pcbMountableBondPadRef MBONDPAD_002) ...)
    ...)

The latter part of this example describes how the mountable bond pad MBONDPAD_002 is being placed on the bare board. It corresponds to part terminal TERM_002.
