Constraints:

1. The following constraint applies to this context: All contexts
A pcbElectricalPartFunction can be referenced at most once by any pcbElectricalPartFunctionPermutable or pcbElectricalPartFunctionNonPermutable constructs in the context of an electrical part.
(See also similar constraint in: pcbElectricalPartFunctionNonPermutable)

2. The following constraint applies to this context: All contexts
At least two items must be referenced by this construct.
(See also similar constraint in: pcbElectricalPartFunctionNonPermutable, pcbElectricalTerminalNonPermutable, pcbElectricalTerminalPermutable)

Description:

Describes a relationship in which functions are interchangeable; for example, AND gates within a packaged or printed electrical part. PcbElectricalPartFunctionPermutable may be nested with pcbElectricalPartFunctionNonPermutable to specify complex relationships between functions in the part.

In the context of a pcbElectricalPartFunctionPermutable construct, corresponding functions may be exchanged as a whole or not at all; partial exchanges are not permitted. For instance, in the second example shown below, E is permutable with F only if G and H are swapped at the same time. As you can see from this example, the order of pcbElectricalPartFunctionRefs within pcbElectricalPartFunctionNonPermutable is significant.

Example:

(pcbElectricalPartFunctionPermutable
   (pcbElectricalPartFunctionNonPermutable
      (pcbElectricalPartFunctionPermutable 
         (pcbElectricalPartFunctionRef A)
         (pcbElectricalPartFunctionRef B))
      (pcbElectricalPartFunctionPermutable
         (pcbElectricalPartFunctionRef C)
         (pcbElectricalPartFunctionRef D))))

This describes the permutability of the following part. A and B are permutable, and C and D are permutable. However, A is not permutable with C and B is not permutable with D. See nonPermutable for a further example.

Example:

(pcbElectricalPartFunctionPermutable
   (pcbElectricalPartFunctionNonPermutable 
      (pcbElectricalPartFunctionRef E)
      (pcbElectricalPartFunctionRef G))
   (pcbElectricalPartFunctionNonPermutable
      (pcbElectricalPartFunctionRef F)
      (pcbElectricalPartFunctionRef H)))

This describes the permutability of the following part. E and G taken together are permutable with F and H taken together. However, E is not permutable with G, nor is F permutable with H. See nonPermutable for a further example.

Constraints:

1. The following constraint applies to this context: All contexts
At least two items must be referenced by this construct.
(See also similar constraint in: pcbElectricalPartFunctionNonPermutable, pcbElectricalPartFunctionPermutable, pcbElectricalTerminalNonPermutable)

2. The following constraint applies to this context: All contexts
A pcbPackagedElectricalPartTerminal or a pcbPrintedElectricalPartTerminal can be referenced at most once by any pcbElectricalTerminalPermutable or pcbElectricalTerminalNonPermutable constructs in the context of an electrical part.
(See also similar constraint in: pcbElectricalTerminalNonPermutable)

Description:

Describes a relationship in which terminals on an electrical part are interchangeable; for example, terminals connected to the same AND gate. PcbElectricalTerminalPermutable may be nested with pcbElectricalTerminalNonPermutable to specify complex relationships between terminals.

In the context of a pcbElectricalTerminalPermutable construct, corresponding functions may be exchanged as a whole or not at all; partial exchanges are not permitted. For instance, in the following example, the pair (T1, T2) are permutable with (T3, T4) or (T7, T6) or (T9, T8). As you can see from this example, the order of pcbElectricalPartTerminalRefs within pcbElectricalTerminalNonPermutable is significant.

Example:

(pcbElectricalTerminalPermutable
   (pcbElectricalTerminalNonPermutable
      (pcbElectricalPartTerminalRef T1)
      (pcbElectricalPartTerminalRef T2))
   (pcbElectricalTerminalNonPermutable
      (pcbElectricalPartTerminalRef T3)
      (pcbElectricalPartTerminalRef T4))
   (pcbElectricalTerminalNonPermutable
      (pcbElectricalPartTerminalRef T7)
      (pcbElectricalPartTerminalRef T6))
   (pcbElectricalTerminalNonPermutable
      (pcbElectricalPartTerminalRef T9)
      (pcbElectricalPartTerminalRef T8)))

This describes the permutability of the following terminals. See nonPermutable for a further example.
