3 Reusable objects

A "reusable object" is an object which is defined once and re-used many times. At present, three types of reusable objects are defined:

  • Hierarchical design objects which correspond to cells.

  • Instantiatable templates which graphically illustrate connectivity structure e.g. offpageconnectors, rippers. Instantiatable templates have specific semantics related to the context in which they are used. These templates are often defined in CAD libraries. One of the characteristics of instantiatable templates is that, unlike cells, they are not hierarchical. Therefore, an instantiatable template cannot itself instantiate other instantiatable templates but it may contain occurrences of reusable dump graphics objects.

Instantiatable templates are viewtype-specific. Various categories of templates are limited to be used within schematic views or PCB/MCM views. Descriptions of instantiatable templates for the schematic domain can be found in chapter 12 and descriptions of instantiatable templates for the PCB/MCM domain can be found in chapter 27.

  • Dumb graphics templateswhich allow graphics to be defined for re-use. A geometry_macro_template is a collection of geometry primitives (see geometry_element in chapter 9). It may contain occurrences of other geometry_macro_templates. It is not viewtype-specific, hence it can be used inside schematic views or PCB/MCM views.
